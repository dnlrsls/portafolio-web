# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated choice: Astro with strict TypeScript, static output, native CSS, and focused
client-side TypeScript/Canvas 2D only where interaction is necessary. Content will be
managed with Astro Content Collections and deployed to GitHub Pages through GitHub
Actions.

## Users

Daniel Rosales (`@dnlrsls`) is the public identity. The primary audiences are
prospective clients interested in freelance services and recruiters evaluating
professional fit. They need to understand the quality of the work, inspect concrete
evidence, and decide whether to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio communicates two connected professional identities: open-source
contributor and freelancer. The initial release presents selected client projects and
verifiable open-source work. Its primary business outcome is generating relevant
freelance inquiries.

The public role line is “Freelance Product Engineer & Open-Source Contributor.”

## Positioning

Public open-source work is not a separate résumé category; it is direct evidence of
technical judgment, collaboration, and the ability to deliver useful work. That
evidence supports the freelance offering and gives clients and recruiters concrete
work to inspect instead of relying on unsupported claims.

## Operating Context

Visitors will typically arrive from GitHub, professional networks, a job or project
conversation, or a shared direct link. They should be able to scan the portfolio,
encounter selected client work before open-source tools grounded in verified work,
follow the original project and repository links, and then contact the owner about freelance work
or professional opportunities. Project exploration remains the primary hero action.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The initial release is bilingual in Spanish and English.
- The portfolio is a single-page experience rather than a multi-page case-study site.
- On a visitor's first visit, the browser language selects Spanish or English. A
  persistent manual language control remains available.
- Selected work physically precedes open-source tools, matching the navigation order
  and primary hero action.
- Exploring projects is the primary first action.
- The initial release includes selected client projects, open-source tools,
  freelance services, and contact information.
- Freelance inquiries can use `danelcan14@gmail.com` or
  `https://www.linkedin.com/in/daniel-rosales00/`.
- The site is statically generated and hosted on GitHub Pages; it has no required
  application backend.
- The repository name and final portfolio URL remain to be confirmed.

## Brand Commitments

- ASCII art is a central identity element, not incidental decoration.
- A portrait of the owner rendered as ASCII is the signature visual asset. Its source
  will be the public avatar at `https://github.com/dnlrsls`.
- The approved dual-theme monochrome editorial direction uses either mineral black
  with warm white or a cold-neutral daylight canvas with dark neutral text. Real
  character compositions carry page structure and hierarchy in both modes.
- A first visit follows the operating-system color preference. A compact persistent
  manual override remains available without affecting the independent language choice.
- The result should feel technical, experimental, and human without becoming a
  generic “hacker” or Matrix imitation.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with inspectable work rather than broad self-promotional claims.
2. Present open-source tools through capability claims grounded in verified work.
3. Make project exploration immediate and conversion to a conversation uncomplicated.
4. Preserve a distinctive identity without sacrificing clarity, speed, or trust.
5. Treat bilingual content as equal product content, not an afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must support keyboard navigation, semantic reading order, sufficient
contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion preferences. ASCII and Canvas-based
art must have meaningful text alternatives and cannot be the only way essential
information is communicated.
